# Sahara Demo Family Clinic (fictional): front-desk information

This clinic, its address, phone numbers, doctors and insurers are invented for a portfolio project.

## hours: Opening hours

- Monday to Friday: 08:00 to 20:00.
- Saturday: 09:00 to 17:00.
- Sunday: closed.
- Blood tests (laboratory desk): Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 12:00.
- On UAE public holidays the clinic is closed; holiday hours are posted on the clinic's channels one week before.

## location: Address and directions

- Address: Unit 4, Building 7, 21A Street, Al Wasl, Dubai (fictional address).
- Parking: free basement parking for patients; take a parking ticket and get it stamped at reception.
- Taxi: tell the driver "Building 7, 21A Street, Al Wasl".
- Access: step-free entrance and a lift to every floor; wheelchairs are available at reception.

## what_to_bring: What to bring to your appointment

- Your Emirates ID (or passport for visitors).
- Your insurance card, if you have insurance.
- Any referral letter and previous reports or test results from other clinics.
- For children: the child's Emirates ID or passport and vaccination card.
- Please arrive 15 minutes before your appointment time to register.

## preparation: Preparation instructions written by the clinic

- Fasting blood tests (for example a lipid profile or fasting glucose): do not eat for 8 hours before the test. Water is allowed. Come to the laboratory desk between 08:00 and 12:00.
- Abdominal ultrasound: do not eat for 6 hours before the scan. Water is allowed.
- Other blood tests: no fasting is needed unless your doctor told you otherwise.
- Questions about your own medicines before a test (whether to take them, or when) must be answered by a nurse or your doctor, not by the assistant. Ask for a nurse call-back.

## insurance_list: Accepted insurance

- Accepted: Gulf Shield Health, Oasis Care, Falcon Cover, Palm Assure, Lotus Mutual Insurance (demo).
- Not accepted: Coral Life, Horizon Plus. Patients with these insurers can be seen as self-pay and claim back from their insurer.
- Self-pay patients get the price list at reception before the consultation.

## preapproval_documents: Insurance pre-approval checklist

Some services need the insurer's pre-approval before the appointment (for example ultrasound, dermatology procedures and some specialist referrals). The clinic sends the request to the insurer for you. Please bring or send:

1. A copy of your Emirates ID (front and back).
2. A copy of your insurance card (front and back).
3. The doctor's request form or referral letter.
4. Any previous reports related to the request.

Pre-approval usually takes 1 to 3 working days. Reception calls you when the insurer answers.

## billing: Payments and invoices

- Payment methods: debit or credit card, Apple Pay, and cash at reception.
- Insured patients pay their co-payment at reception after the consultation.
- An itemised invoice can be emailed to the address in your file on request.
- Overpayments are refunded to the original card within 14 days.
- Questions about a specific bill go to the billing team by call-back.

## booking_policy: Booking, rescheduling and cancelling

- Appointments are 30 minutes and can be booked up to 3 weeks ahead.
- Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before the appointment.
- If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, reception may move you to the next free time.
- To book, reschedule, cancel or check results status, the assistant first checks your file number and date of birth.

## lab_results: Lab results

- Results are usually ready 1 to 3 working days after the test.
- The assistant can only tell you whether a result is ready, never what it means.
- Ready results can be viewed in the patient portal or collected at reception with your Emirates ID.
- Your doctor explains your results at a follow-up appointment or by phone.

## contact: Talking to a person

- Phone: +971 4 000 0000 (fake number), during opening hours.
- Type "staff" in this chat to ask for a person; reception replies during opening hours.
- In an emergency, call 998 (ambulance) or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for a reply in this chat.

## privacy: Your data

- The assistant is an AI. It handles bookings and admin questions only and does not give medical advice.
- It checks your file number and date of birth before showing anything about your file and never shares another patient's details.
- Chat logs keep only what is needed for admin (no symptoms or free text in the audit log).
